Community Guidelines

Effective: August 2026

Pink.TT exists so that women can move around Trinidad and Tobago safely. These Guidelines describe the conduct expected of every user of the Platform, rider and driver alike.

1. Respect and Safety

  • Every rider and driver is to be treated with courtesy and respect.
  • Harassment, threats, or discriminatory conduct of any kind are prohibited.
  • Unwanted contact outside the Platform, using information shared for the purpose of a ride, is prohibited.

2. Honesty

  • Accurate information must be provided at registration, including for identity verification and driver documentation.
  • Ratings and reports must reflect genuine experience. False reviews and retaliatory reporting are prohibited.

3. Use of the SOS Feature

The SOS feature is intended for genuine emergencies. It alerts the Pink.TT safety team immediately and, where configured, places a call or SMS to that team. It is to be reserved for situations in which a user feels unsafe or is in danger. Misuse may result in account suspension.

4. Zero Tolerance

The following will result in immediate account review and may result in permanent suspension:

  • Any form of physical or sexual harassment.
  • Providing false identity information to circumvent the Platform's women-only verification.
  • Driving under the influence, or any conduct that endangers the safety of a rider.
  • Repeated, confirmed complaints from other users.

5. Reporting a Concern

The in-application "Report Issue" feature, or the support email address published within the application, should be used to raise any concern regarding safety or conduct. Reports are reviewed by the Pink.TT administrative team.

These Guidelines apply together with the Pink.TT Terms of Service and, for drivers, the Driver Agreement.
