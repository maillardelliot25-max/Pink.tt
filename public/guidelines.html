Community Guidelines

Last updated: August 2026

Pink.TT exists so women can move around Trinidad & Tobago safely. These guidelines describe the behaviour expected of everyone on the platform, riders and drivers alike.

1. Respect & Safety First

  • Treat every rider and driver with courtesy and respect.
  • No harassment, threats, or discriminatory behaviour of any kind.
  • No unwanted contact outside the platform using information shared for a ride.

2. Honesty

  • Provide accurate information at registration, including for identity verification and driver documentation.
  • Ratings and reports should reflect genuine experiences — no false reviews or retaliatory reporting.

3. Using SOS Responsibly

SOS is for genuine emergencies. It immediately alerts our safety team (and, where configured, places a real call/SMS to them) — please reserve it for situations where you feel unsafe or are in danger. Misuse may result in account suspension.

4. Zero Tolerance

The following result in immediate account review and possible permanent suspension:

  • Any form of physical or sexual harassment.
  • Providing false identity information to bypass the platform's women-only verification.
  • Driving under the influence, or any conduct that endangers a rider's safety.
  • Repeated confirmed complaints from other users.

5. Reporting a Problem

Use the in-app "Report Issue" feature after a ride, or contact the support email shown in the app, to flag anything that made you feel unsafe or uncomfortable. Reports are reviewed by our admin team.

These guidelines work alongside our Terms of Service and, for drivers, the Driver Agreement.
