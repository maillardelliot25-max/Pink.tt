Privacy Policy

Effective: August 2026  ·  Trinidad & Tobago

Pink.TT ("we", "our", "the Platform") is a women-only rideshare service operating in Trinidad & Tobago. This Policy describes what personal data is collected, why it is collected, and the rights an individual has over that data.

1. Information Collected

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, and password (stored as a one-way hash and never in plain text).
  • Identity verification photo: a photo submitted at registration to confirm eligibility for a women-only platform. This photo is transmitted to our verification provider for a one-time automated check and is not retained by Pink.TT following that check; only the resulting outcome is retained.
  • Driver documents: for driver accounts, the driver's licence photo and vehicle details, retained for administrative review and compliance while the account remains active.
  • Location data: pickup and destination addresses, live GPS coordinates during an active ride, and location at the moment an SOS alert is triggered.
  • Emergency contact details: name and phone number voluntarily provided, used only in connection with an SOS alert or a shared trip.
  • Ride and payment history: fares, ride records, driver earnings, and loyalty programme activity.
  • Technical data: IP address and basic request logs, used for security purposes including rate limiting and fraud prevention.

2. Use of Information

  • To create and secure user accounts, and to match riders with drivers.
  • To verify eligibility for this women-only platform.
  • To operate safety features, including SOS alerts to the safety team and, where provided, an emergency contact.
  • To calculate fares, process driver earnings, and maintain ride history.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, including rate-limiting of login attempts.

3. Disclosure to Third Parties

Pink.TT does not sell personal data. Limited data is shared with the following categories of service provider, solely to operate the Platform:

  • Identity verification provider: processes the identity verification photo at registration; the photo is not retained after the check.
  • Hosting and database providers: store account and ride data securely.
  • Messaging providers, where configured: used to alert the safety team when SOS is triggered.

4. Data Retention

Account and ride data is retained for as long as the account remains active, and for a reasonable period thereafter for legal, safety, and accounting purposes. Identity verification photos are not retained following the automated check. An account holder may request deletion of their account and associated data at any time (see Section 6).

5. The SOS Feature: Limitations

Triggering SOS logs the alert and notifies the Pink.TT safety team, and, where configured, places an automated call or SMS to a safety-team phone number. It does not automatically dispatch police. A human member of the safety team reviews each alert and determines whether to contact the Trinidad and Tobago Police Service. In the event of immediate danger, emergency services should be contacted directly.

6. Rights of the Individual

An individual may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal data, subject to applicable legal and safety retention requirements, by contacting the support email address published within the application. Such requests are handled in a manner consistent with the Trinidad and Tobago Data Protection Act.

7. Security

Pink.TT employs industry-standard measures including password hashing, encrypted connections, and access controls to protect personal data. No system is entirely secure, and use of a strong, unique password is recommended.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Policy may be updated as the Platform evolves. Material changes will be reflected by an update to the effective date above.

9. Contact

Questions regarding this Policy or the handling of personal data may be directed to the support email address published within the application.
