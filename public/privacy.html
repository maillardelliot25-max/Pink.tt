Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · Trinidad & Tobago

Pink.TT ("we", "our", "the platform") is a women-only rideshare service operating in Trinidad & Tobago. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and what rights you have over it.

1. What We Collect

  • Account information: name, email, phone number, password (stored as a one-way hash, never in plain text).
  • Identity verification photo: a photo you submit at registration to confirm eligibility for a women-only platform. This photo is sent to our verification provider for a one-time automated check and is not stored by Pink.TT after that check completes — we retain only the pass/fail result.
  • Driver documents: if you register as a driver, your driver's licence photo and vehicle details, retained for admin review and compliance while your driver account is active.
  • Location data: pickup/destination addresses and live GPS coordinates during an active ride, and your location at the moment you trigger an SOS alert.
  • Emergency contact details: name and phone number you voluntarily provide, used only if you trigger SOS or share a trip.
  • Ride & payment history: fares, ride records, driver earnings, and PinkPoints activity.
  • Technical data: IP address and basic request logs, used for security (e.g. rate limiting, fraud prevention).

2. How We Use It

  • To create and secure your account, and match riders with drivers.
  • To verify eligibility for this women-only platform.
  • To operate safety features, including SOS alerts to our safety team and, where you've provided one, your emergency contact.
  • To calculate fares, process driver earnings, and maintain ride history.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse (e.g. rate-limiting login attempts).

3. Who We Share Data With

We do not sell your personal data. We share limited data with the following service providers, solely to operate the platform:

  • Anthropic — processes your identity verification photo at registration (not stored after the check).
  • Our hosting and database providers — store your account and ride data securely.
  • SMS/voice provider (where configured) — used to alert our safety team automatically when you trigger SOS.

4. Data Retention

We retain account and ride data for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward for legal, safety, and accounting purposes. Identity verification photos are not retained after the automated check completes. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see Section 6).

5. Emergency (SOS) Feature — Important Limitations

Pressing SOS logs your alert and notifies the Pink.TT safety team (and, where configured, places an automated call/SMS to a real safety-team phone number). It does not automatically dispatch police. A human member of our safety team reviews each alert and decides whether to contact the Trinidad & Tobago Police Service. If you are in immediate danger, contact emergency services directly as well.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, subject to legal and safety retention requirements, by contacting us at the support email listed in the app. We aim to handle such requests in line with the Trinidad & Tobago Data Protection Act.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures including password hashing, encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access controls to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure, and we encourage you to use a strong, unique password.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the platform evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to the support email address shown in the app.
