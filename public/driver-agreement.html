Driver Agreement

Effective: August 2026  ·  Governing Law: Trinidad & Tobago

This Agreement applies in addition to the Pink.TT Terms of Service, specifically to accounts registered as drivers on the Platform.

1. Independent Contractor Status

A Pink.TT driver operates as an independent contractor, and is not an employee, agent, or partner of Pink.TT. The driver controls their own schedule, selects which ride requests to accept, and provides their own vehicle. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship.

2. Eligibility and Requirements

  • The driver must hold a valid driver's licence for Trinidad and Tobago and submit a clear photograph of it during registration.
  • The driver must own, or have lawful use of, a roadworthy and appropriately insured vehicle, and must provide accurate vehicle details, including make, model, year, colour, and registration plate.
  • The driver is solely responsible for maintaining any licence, permit, insurance, or registration required by law to operate as a for-hire or transport-network driver in Trinidad and Tobago.
  • Applications are subject to administrative review. Pink.TT may approve, reject, or subsequently suspend a driver account at its discretion, including on grounds of safety concern or incomplete or inaccurate documentation.

3. Fares and Earnings

Fares are calculated automatically according to Pink.TT's published rate structure and are shown to the rider prior to booking. Earnings per completed ride, and the applicable platform fee, are recorded in the driver's account. Payment and cashout mechanics are as described within the application.

4. Conduct

  • The driver shall treat riders respectfully and shall prioritise rider safety.
  • The driver shall not discriminate against a rider on any unlawful basis.
  • The driver shall keep vehicle and documentation information accurate and current within the application.
  • Serious or repeated complaints, safety incidents, or falsified information may result in suspension or removal from the Platform.

5. Insurance and Liability

Pink.TT does not provide vehicle insurance, health insurance, or workers' compensation coverage for drivers. The driver is responsible for maintaining insurance appropriate to for-hire driving under the laws of Trinidad and Tobago. Pink.TT is not liable for accidents, injuries, or losses arising from the driver's operation of their vehicle.

6. Termination

The driver may cease driving on Pink.TT at any time. Pink.TT may suspend or terminate a driver account for violation of this Agreement or the Terms of Service, for safety concerns, or otherwise at its discretion with reasonable cause.

7. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Trinidad and Tobago.
