Driver Agreement

Last updated: August 2026 · Governing law: Trinidad & Tobago

This Agreement applies in addition to the general Terms of Service, specifically to accounts registered as drivers on Pink.TT.

1. Independent Contractor Status

As a Pink.TT driver, you operate as an independent contractor, not an employee, agent, or partner of Pink.TT. You control your own schedule, choose which ride requests to accept, and use your own vehicle. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship.

2. Eligibility & Requirements

  • You must hold a valid driver's licence for Trinidad & Tobago and submit a clear photo of it during registration.
  • You must own or have lawful use of a roadworthy, appropriately insured vehicle, and provide accurate vehicle details (make, model, year, colour, plate).
  • You are solely responsible for maintaining any licence, permit, insurance, or registration required by law to operate as a for-hire/transport-network driver in Trinidad & Tobago.
  • Your application is subject to admin review. Pink.TT may approve, reject, or later suspend your driver account at its discretion, including for safety concerns or incomplete/inaccurate documentation.

3. Fares & Earnings

Fares are calculated automatically using Pink.TT's published rate structure and shown to the rider before booking. Your earnings per completed ride, and the platform's fee, are shown in your driver dashboard. Payment/cashout mechanics are as described in the app.

4. Conduct

  • You agree to treat riders respectfully and to prioritize their safety.
  • You agree not to discriminate against riders on any unlawful basis.
  • You agree to keep your vehicle and documentation information accurate and up to date in the app.
  • Serious or repeated complaints, safety incidents, or falsified information may result in suspension or removal from the platform.

5. Insurance & Liability

Pink.TT does not provide vehicle insurance, health insurance, or workers' compensation coverage for drivers. You are responsible for maintaining insurance appropriate to for-hire driving under Trinidad & Tobago law. Pink.TT is not liable for accidents, injuries, or losses arising from your operation of your vehicle.

6. Termination

You may stop driving on Pink.TT at any time. Pink.TT may suspend or terminate your driver account for violations of this Agreement, the general Terms of Service, safety concerns, or at its discretion with reasonable cause.

7. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Trinidad & Tobago.
