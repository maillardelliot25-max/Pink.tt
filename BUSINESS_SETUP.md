# Pink.TT — Business Registration Guide (Trinidad & Tobago)

This is a reference document for you, not published on the live site. It's a starting
guide based on current public information about T&T business registration — not a
substitute for an accountant or attorney, especially given the transport-specific
licensing note in Section 3.

## 1. Choose a business structure

| | Sole Trader | Limited Liability Company (Ltd) |
|---|---|---|
| Setup cost | Cheapest, fastest | ~TTD $505 average |
| Time to register | Days | 3–6 weeks |
| Liability | **You are personally liable** for business debts/claims | Personal assets protected from business debts/claims |
| Requirements | Minimal — individual registration | 2 directors + 2 shareholders (at least one with a local registered address) |
| Tax | Taxed as personal income | Corporate tax rate 25%, VAT 12.5% |

**Worth thinking about specifically for Pink.TT:** this platform handles SOS/emergency
data, driver vetting, and eventually real payments — all things that carry real legal
exposure if something goes wrong. A Sole Trader structure means *you personally* are on
the hook for claims against the business. Many platforms in this position choose Ltd
specifically for that liability separation, but it costs more and takes longer to set
up. That trade-off is yours to weigh, not something I can decide for you.

## 2. Registration process

1. Business/company registration in T&T runs through the **Companies Registry**
   (Ministry of Legal Affairs, Registrar General's Department).
2. Two online portals handle this: **CROS** (Companies Registry Online System) for
   standard registration, and **TTBizLink** for broader business services.
3. You'll need: government-issued ID, proof of address, a name-availability check/
   reservation, completed registration forms, and the registration fee.
4. For an Ltd company specifically: director and shareholder identification, a
   registered business address, and (per current sources) notarization of documents.

## 3. Transport-specific licensing — separate process

This is the part most likely to be a real bottleneck: **transport/rideshare businesses
require additional approval outside the standard CROS registration**, handled through
the relevant line ministry or local government rather than the Companies Registry
itself. This is not something available to research definitively from outside — you'll
need to contact the relevant ministry (likely the Ministry of Works and Transport, or
whichever body currently handles for-hire vehicle/transport network licensing) directly
to find out exactly what's required for a rideshare platform specifically, since this
differs from e.g. registering a taxi company or a private hire vehicle individually.
**Do not treat Pink.TT as fully legally operating "for real" until this piece is
confirmed** — it's the one requirement that's genuinely outside what registration
paperwork alone covers.

## 4. What this doesn't cover

- Tax registration (BIR file number, VAT registration if you cross the threshold)
- Whether drivers need their own individual for-hire permits/badges in addition to
  the platform's own licensing
- Insurance requirements (both for the business and for drivers' vehicles)
- Data protection registration obligations, if any, under the T&T Data Protection Act

All of the above are exactly the kind of thing to bring to an accountant and a lawyer
who work in T&T — I can get you general, publicly-known facts, but I can't file
paperwork, contact government ministries on your behalf, or give you a legally
reliable final answer on jurisdiction-specific licensing.
